LAB 4 Q3 - ALU flags and conditional jumps

The Hack ALU provides:
zr = 1 exactly when the 16-bit ALU output is 0.
ng = 1 when the output's most significant bit is 1 (two's-complement negative interpretation).

Conditional jump decoding:
JGT (jump field 001): jump when ng=0 and zr=0.
JEQ (010): jump when zr=1.
JGE (011): jump when ng=1 OR zr=1.
JLT (100): jump when ng=1.
JNE (101): jump when zr=0.
JLE (110): jump when ng=1 OR zr=1.
JMP (111): unconditional jump.

Examples:
D=5; D;JGT -> jump is taken.
D=0; D;JEQ -> jump is taken.
D=-1; D;JLT -> jump is taken.

If zr is stuck low, JEQ can never branch and JNE may branch incorrectly.
If ng is inverted, JLT/JGT decisions are reversed for negative/positive outputs.
If jump bits are mapped incorrectly, loops and if/else control flow may fail or run forever.
