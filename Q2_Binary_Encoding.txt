Lab 3 Q2 - Hand encoding examples

A-instruction format:
0 vvvvvvvvvvvvvvv  (15-bit non-negative value)

Example 1:
Assembly: @21
Binary:   0000000000010101
Breakdown: opcode=0, value=000000000010101 (decimal 21)

C-instruction format:
111 a c1c2c3c4c5c6 d1d2d3 j1j2j3

Example 2:
Assembly: D=A
Binary:   1110110000010000
Breakdown:
opcode=111
a=0
comp=110000 (A)
dest=010 (D)
jump=000

Example 3:
Assembly: D=D+A
Binary:   1110000010010000
Breakdown:
opcode=111
a=0
comp=000010 (D+A)
dest=010 (D)
jump=000

Note: The six-bit comp field above follows the standard Hack instruction encoding.
